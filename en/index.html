Three ways to use it

Makeup

Tell GLÓW your skin tone, eye colour, experience level and how bold you want to go. It reads your photo and suggests shades, placement and a step-by-step routine that actually fits your face rather than a generic tutorial.

Style

Photograph an outfit or build a wardrobe photo bank. GLÓW judges what works, what to swap, and puts together combinations for the occasion you name — a wedding, an interview, an ordinary Tuesday.

Professional

Frequently asked

Do I need to install anything?

No. GLÓW runs in the browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

What if I don't like the analysis?

The first three are free precisely so you can find out before paying anything. Recommendations are informational — GLÓW is not a substitute for a dermatologist, and it is not liable for decisions made on the basis of an analysis.

Which languages does it work in?

English and Estonian. The interface switches with the flag buttons on the opening screen.
